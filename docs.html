01 · OVERVIEWWhat is Laptop Shopping Network

A 24 hour shopping channel with one item in the catalogue. The item is the laptop. The host has been presenting it since the tape started and has not blinked.

Everything looks like a VHS recording from 1996 because it is one. Tracking bars, tape wobble and channel static are part of the broadcast, not bugs.

HOST
One man in a blue suit, big grin, both hands always pointing at the laptop
ITEM
Item 0001, a beige laptop, one previous owner, still boots
THE FORMAT
Infomercial. Call now, operators are standing by, only 999 left
CHAIN
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad

02 · THE ITEMWhat you get

The full pitch, as read on air. The counter in the corner is real in the sense that it goes down.

item 0001 ├── ONE LAPTOP one owner, lightly used, heavily discussed ├── STILL BOOTS nobody knows why, nobody has asked ├── CONTENTS not included, already leaked ├── THE STORY you have heard it, it comes with the box ├── ONLY 999 LEFT the counter restocks when it runs out └── BUT WAIT there is no more

03 · ON AIRThe channels

The broadcast cycles through five channels on its own. You can switch with the remote in the corner or with the keyboard.

channels ├── CH 1 the host, live, ten seconds on a loop ├── CH 2 the item on the pedestal, slow zoom ├── CH 3 the host holds it up, the sparkle happens ├── CH 4 Linda from Ohio, before and after └── CH 5 the order form, the contract, the number to call

04 · CONTROLSUsing the site

REMOTE
The - and + buttons in the corner change the channel. ← → and 1 to 5 on the keyboard do the same
CALL NOW
The red button is the buy button. It opens the Pons launchpad, and the token page once the contract is in
SOUND
Quiet until you tap. Your first click anywhere starts the studio music
BELOW THE TV
What you get, satisfied customers, and the order form again with the contract and the buttons
THE COUNTER
Goes down on its own. When it reaches the last few, the warehouse restocks to 999

06 · PRIVACYNothing is stored

No backend, no account, no cookies, nothing written to your browser. The phone lines are a drawing.

MUSIC
